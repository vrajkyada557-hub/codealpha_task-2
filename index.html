What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations to steal usernames, passwords, banking details, and OTPs.

Recognizing Phishing Emails

Suspicious Sender

Fake emails often use domains like: amaz0n-security.com

Urgent Messages

Attackers create panic: “Your account will expire today!”

Grammar Mistakes

Many phishing emails contain poor grammar and spelling mistakes.

How to Detect Fake Websites

  • Check HTTPS security lock
  • Verify correct domain spelling
  • Avoid suspicious download buttons
  • Never share OTP or passwords
Example:

Real: amazon.in

Fake: amaz0n-login-security.com

Social Engineering Tactics

Fear

“Your bank account is blocked!”

Greed

“You won a free iPhone!”

Urgency

“Act within 5 minutes!”

Best Practices

Use strong passwords
Enable 2FA authentication
Never click unknown links
Keep antivirus updated

Real-World Phishing Cases

  • Fake Paytm KYC updates
  • UPI reward scams
  • Bank verification fraud
